# Synthetic development fixtures

`session/` is a small authorized **synthetic** schema-1 session generated by
`python scripts/terrain_perception.py fixtures --layout all --output NEW_PATH`.
It has analytic metric floor/raised/depression geometry, a visible drop face,
missing depth, and a wrong-plane case. Its calibration is synthetic and cannot
be used for hardware. Raw depth is lossless uint16 millimetres; zeros are invalid.

Annotations are geometric definitions independent of detector outputs. The
visible extent can be smaller than the fixture footprint due to occlusion.
These seven development frames are not a real recorded-session regression or
independent physical qualification. Follow `docs/PHASE1_CALIBRATION.md` to collect
the missing measured sessions. No private/captured camera images are included.
